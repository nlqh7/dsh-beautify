fix: WE picker drop big preview + maid no longer covers the WE wallpaper layer

- WallpaperEngineBlock: remove the modalPreview (large preview image at the
  top of the picker); the tile grid stays.
- Root cause of "wallpaper switch does nothing": maid-atelier sets an
  opaque body background-color (#dce6f5) that painted over the WE
  wallpaper layer even though body[data-we-wallpaper] makes the --dsw-alias
  tokens transparent. Add
  body[data-dsh-maid-atelier][data-we-wallpaper] { background-color:
  transparent !important; } so the selected wallpaper actually shows.

Verified in playwright: picker has no modalPreview; after clicking the
first tile (web wallpaper) the WE layer exists with an iframe and the
maid body background is transparent, so the wallpaper is visible.
